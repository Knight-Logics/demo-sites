Every year from June through November, Florida braces for hurricane season. Your roof is your home's first line of defense against wind, rain, and flying debris. A little preparation now can prevent catastrophic damage — and costly emergency repairs — when a storm makes landfall.

Schedule a Pre-Season Inspection

A professional inspection identifies loose shingles, deteriorated flashing, clogged gutters, and weak points you can't see from the ground. Addressing these issues before storm season is far less expensive than emergency tarping and interior water damage after the fact.

Trim Trees and Clear Debris

Overhanging branches can scrape and puncture roofing materials in high winds. Clear gutters and downspouts so heavy rain drains properly instead of backing up under shingles or into fascia boards.

Document Your Roof's Condition

Take dated photos of your roof and attic before hurricane season. If storm damage occurs, documentation helps streamline insurance claims and proves pre-existing versus storm-related damage.
